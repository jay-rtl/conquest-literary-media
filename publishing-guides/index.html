Publishing Guides

A thoughtful perspective on the journey from manuscript to marketplace.

Talk with our team ↗

Publishing a book involves multiple steps, decisions, and technical requirements. At Conquest Literary Media, our Publishing Guide is designed to help authors better understand the general publishing journey—from manuscript preparation through marketplace readiness. Whether you are a first-time author or preparing a new release, having a clear roadmap can help you move forward with greater confidence and organization.

1. Manuscript Preparation

Before entering production, manuscripts typically benefit from careful review, editing consideration, and structural evaluation. Ensuring clarity and readiness at this stage helps support smoother progress later in the process.

2. Formatting & Design

Professional interior formatting and appropriate cover design help ensure your book meets common publishing standards and presents well to readers and retailers. Key focus areas include: readable interior layout consistent typography proper trim and margins genre-appropriate cover presentation

3. ISBN & Copyright Setup

Books prepared for distribution typically require proper identification and documentation. ISBN assignment and copyright preparation help establish ownership records and support retail compatibility.

4. Pre-Launch Preparation

Before release, authors often benefit from early positioning efforts. Preparing marketing assets, author platforms, and promotional plans in advance can help support a more organized launch.

5. Sales & Retail Readiness

Proper configuration for distribution channels helps ensure your book is positioned for marketplace visibility. This includes metadata accuracy, product page setup, and retail alignment.

6. Ongoing Promotion & Visibility

After launch, continued visibility efforts may help maintain reader awareness and support long-term discoverability through digital promotion and public-facing activities.

Our Role at Conquest Literary Media

At Conquest Literary Media, we focus on helping authors navigate these stages with clarity and structured support. Our services are designed to assist with preparation, coordination, and professional positioning while maintaining realistic expectations throughout the publishing process.

Helpful Tips for Authors

Authors preparing for publication may benefit from:
  • allowing sufficient time for preparation
  • maintaining organized manuscript files
  • understanding their target audience
  • approaching publication as both creative and strategic
  • seeking professional guidance when needed
