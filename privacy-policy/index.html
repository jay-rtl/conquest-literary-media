Privacy Policy

Conquest Literary Media

Effective Date: February 1, 2024

Conquest Literary Media (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you may provide through our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect certain information when you interact with our website or contact us through forms, email, or other communication channels.

This information may include:

Name

Email address

Phone number

Information you voluntarily submit through contact forms or inquiries

Website usage information such as browser type, device type, and pages visited

We collect only the information necessary to provide services, respond to inquiries, and improve the functionality of our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our website may be used for the following purposes:

Responding to inquiries or service requests

Providing information about our publishing or marketing services

Communicating with authors regarding projects or consultations

Improving the performance and usability of our website

Maintaining website security and preventing misuse

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve the browsing experience and understand how visitors interact with the site.

Cookies may help us:

analyze website traffic

improve website performance

understand visitor behavior

Users may disable cookies through their browser settings if they prefer not to allow this functionality.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites for informational purposes. These external websites are not operated or controlled by Conquest Literary Media.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy policies of those websites before providing personal information.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information submitted through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed toward individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that information from a child has been submitted, we will take reasonable steps to remove that information.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us at any time to:

request information about data we may hold about you

request correction or removal of personal information submitted through the website

opt out of communications

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Conquest Literary Media may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Updated versions will be posted on this page with the revised effective date.
