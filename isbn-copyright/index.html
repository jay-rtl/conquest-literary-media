Protect. Identify. Publish with Confidence.
Establishing proper identification and legal protection is an essential step in the publishing process. At Conquest Literary Media, our ISBN & Copyright Support service helps authors prepare the foundational elements needed for professional book distribution and ownership documentation. We guide authors through the technical requirements so their work is properly registered, organized, and ready for marketplace participation.

What This Service Includes

Our support process is designed to simplify key publishing requirements:

  • ISBN assignment guidance
  • Barcode generation for print editions
  • Copyright page preparation
  • Publishing metadata organization
  • Ownership documentation support
  • Platform-ready identifier setup
Our team focuses on accuracy and proper configuration to help reduce common setup issues during distribution.

Why ISBN & Copyright Matter

ISBNs serve as the primary identification system used by retailers, distributors, and libraries worldwide. Proper copyright documentation helps establish public record of authorship and publication details. Completing these steps correctly helps ensure:

  • smoother distribution workflows
  • clearer book identification
  • improved retail compatibility
  • stronger professional presentation

Our Approach

At Conquest Literary Media, we focus on clarity, accuracy, and coordination. Our role is to help authors navigate the technical requirements of ISBN assignment and copyright preparation while maintaining clear expectations throughout the process.

Who This Service Is For

This service is ideal for:

  • first-time self-publishing authors
  • writers preparing print and ebook releases
  • authors updating previously published titles
  • professionals seeking properly documented works
